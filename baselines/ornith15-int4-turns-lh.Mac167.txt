A mutex (short for "mutual exclusion") is a synchronization primitive that ensures only one thread or process can access a shared resource or critical section of code at a time, preventing concurrent access and the data corruption that could result.
--- follow-up ---
A semaphore is a synchronization primitive that uses a counter to control access to a shared resource, allowing a limited number of threads to access it simultaneously (unlike a mutex, which restricts access to just one).
