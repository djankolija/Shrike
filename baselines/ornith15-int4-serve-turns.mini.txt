A mutex (short for "mutual exclusion") is a synchronization primitive that ensures only one thread or process can access a particular resource or critical section of code at a time, preventing concurrent access conflicts.
--- follow-up ---
A semaphore is a synchronization primitive that uses a counter to control access to a shared resource, allowing either a fixed number of threads to access it concurrently (counting semaphore) or just one thread at a time (binary semaphore), enabling both mutual exclusion and coordination between threads.
