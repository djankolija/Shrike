A mutex (short for "mutual exclusion") is a synchronization primitive used in concurrent programming to protect shared resources from being accessed by multiple threads simultaneously. It ensures that only one thread can access a particular resource at a time, preventing race conditions and data corruption. You would use a mutex when you need to coordinate access to shared data structures, files, or other resources that could be modified by multiple threads.
